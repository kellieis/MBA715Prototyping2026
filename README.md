MBA716 is the integrating course of Module II of the Digital Product Management MBA at Estonian Business School. Three parallel courses feed into this one project:

|---|---|---|

You work **individually** on your own product. In class, you work in groups of 4–5 on one group member's project at a time, using the same templates you then fill in at home.

The course ends with the **defense on Saturday 12 December 2026**, where you pitch and demo your tested product to a panel acting as an executive team.

> **DownUnder Compass is a relocation platform that gives Estonians moving to Australia for study or work one personalised, verified plan covering visas, city choice, housing, jobs, banking and costs, so they pay to save weeks of research and avoid costly mistakes.**

Student: Kelli Eismann

Live prototype: [URL – not yet built]

| | |
|---|---|
| **Target customer** | Estonian students and young professionals aged 18–35 planning to move to Australia for at least six months for study or work (e.g. Working Holiday or Student visa), mainly to Sydney, Melbourne or Brisbane. Later expansion: other Baltic and European countries. |
| **Problem** | Reliable, up-to-date information on visas, education, jobs, accommodation, choosing a city, opening a bank account and relocation costs is scattered across many websites. Planning takes weeks of research, is stressful, and gaps in information lead to costly mistakes. |
| **Current workaround** | Googling official Australian government sites, university and agency websites, asking in Facebook groups for Estonians in Australia, and asking friends who have already moved. |
| **Solution** | A platform with free basic relocation guides plus a premium AI-powered assistant that builds a personalised relocation plan (checklist, budget, deadline reminders) based on the user's purpose, budget and destination, using information verified against official sources. Service providers can advertise to this audience through a business dashboard. |
| **Who pays, how much, how often** | (1) Users: premium subscription, approx. €5–10/month, for personalised plans, checklists, budget planning, English courses and reminders. (2) Businesses (universities and colleges, recruitment agencies, visa lawyers, accommodation, insurance, travel and relocation companies): paid advertising, sponsored listings and referral fees. |
| **Evidence of willingness to pay** | Not yet collected. Planned interviews: Estonians who have lived in Australia on Working Holiday or Student visas; Estonians currently planning a move (via Facebook groups, university networks); exchange office coordinators at University of Tartu, EBS, TalTech and Tallinn University; Estonian travel agencies. |
| **User roles** | User (free and premium – students and young professionals), Service provider / Advertiser, Admin |
